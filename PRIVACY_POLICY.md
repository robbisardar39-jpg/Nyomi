# Privacy Policy for Nyomi

Last updated: November 20, 2026

**Nyomi** ("we", "our", or "the App") is an open-source manga, manhwa, and comic reader application forked from Komikku. This Privacy Policy explains how Nyomi handles information when you use the App.

## 1. Information We Collect and Store
- **Local App Data:** Your library, reading history, categories, settings, and downloaded or local files are stored locally on your device. Nyomi does not require an account to use core reading features.
- **Google Drive Sync (Optional):** If you choose to enable Google Drive synchronization, Nyomi uses Google OAuth and requests Google Drive access (`drive.file` and `drive.appdata`) solely to create, read, update, and delete Nyomi sync/backup data in your own Google Drive account. OAuth tokens are stored locally on your device.
- **Third-Party Sources and Trackers:** Nyomi itself does not host content. When you add external extension repositories, browse sources, or connect third-party tracking/sync services, your requests and credentials interact directly with those respective third-party services under their own policies.

## 2. How We Use Information
- To provide local reading, backup/restore, and optional Google Drive sync functionality.
- To maintain and improve Nyomi's features and stability.

## 3. Data Sharing and Sale
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Data synced to Google Drive goes directly to your own Google Drive account via Google's APIs.

## 4. Your Choices and Controls
- You can disconnect Google Drive sync or purge Nyomi's Google Drive sync data from the App's settings or revoke access in your Google Account permissions at any time.
- You can clear local App data or uninstall Nyomi at any time.

## 5. Contact
For questions about this Privacy Policy or Nyomi, please open an issue on the project repository: `https://github.com/robbisardar39-jpg/Nyomi`.
